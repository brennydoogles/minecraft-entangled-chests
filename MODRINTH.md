# Entangled Chests

**Store items from anywhere.** Entangled Chests adds a bundle you can link to a chest — anything you drop into the bundle is teleported straight into its linked chest, no matter how far away you are. Think of it as a personal chute to your base storage.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Entangled Bundle** — a bundle that beams its contents into a linked chest.
- **Entangled Chest** — a chest with a unique identity that bundles can lock onto. Name it on an anvil to tell your chests apart.
- **Entanglement Crystal** — the crafting ingredient that powers it all.
- Link **as many bundles as you like** to a single chest.
- Bundles remember their link across saves, dimensions, and even while you're offline.
- Subtle **enchantment glint** so you can tell at a glance what's linked.

---

## 📖 How to use it

### 1. Craft an Entanglement Crystal

An **Echo Shard** surrounded by **Lapis Lazuli**:

```
Lapis  Lapis  Lapis
Lapis  Echo   Lapis
Lapis  Lapis  Lapis
```

### 2. Craft an Entangled Chest

A normal **Chest** recipe with an **Entanglement Crystal** in the middle:

```
Planks  Planks   Planks
Planks  Crystal  Planks
Planks  Planks   Planks
```

### 3. Craft an Entangled Bundle

A normal **Bundle** recipe (String over Leather) with an **Entanglement Crystal** added in the middle:

```
String
Crystal
Leather
```

### 4. Link and go

1. **Place your Entangled Chest.** When first placed it already contains one Entangled Bundle, pre-linked to itself — grab it and go, or link more later.
2. **Link a bundle:** hold an *unlinked* Entangled Bundle and **right-click the chest**. The bundle now glints and its tooltip shows the chest it's linked to.
3. **Store from anywhere:** put items into the linked bundle just like a normal bundle (drop a stack onto it). They're whisked into the chest instantly. Head out mining and deposit ore into your base chest without a single trip home.

## 🔎 Good to know

- **Unlinked bundles are just normal bundles** — carry items, take them out, whatever you like. The magic starts once you link one to a chest.
- **You can't pull items *out* of a linked bundle by hand** — its contents belong to the chest now. (Break the chest and the bundle unlinks, freeing anything still inside.)
- **If the linked chest is full,** items wait in the bundle until space opens up.
- **Destroying or picking up a chest** instantly unlinks every bundle tied to it — even ones in other players' inventories, online or not.
- **Name a chest on an anvil** (before placing it) and linked bundles will show that name instead of an ID — handy for "Ores", "Food", "Junk", and so on.
- Each bundle links to **one** chest at a time. Link it to a new chest to move it.

## ❓ FAQ

**Do items get lost if the chest fills up?**
No. They stay safe in the bundle and flow into the chest as soon as room frees up.

**What happens to a linked bundle if I break its chest?**
It unlinks and becomes a normal bundle again. Anything buffered inside becomes retrievable.

**Can several bundles feed the same chest?**
Yes — link as many as you want to one chest.

**Does linking work across dimensions?**
Yes. A bundle stays linked to its chest wherever you both are.

**Can I rename a chest after placing it?**
Naming is done on the item before placement. To rename, break the chest, rename the item on an anvil, and place it again (this makes a fresh chest).
